Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Advisory Questions

Clear answers about domestic shipping timelines, trial periods, and equipment maintenance.

What are the shipping timelines for orders across India?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are dispatched the same day from our Chennai hub. Deliveries within South India (Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kochi) typically arrive within 24 to 48 hours. Shipments to Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR, and Kolkata take 2 to 4 business days via BlueDart and Delhivery Express. We provide live tracking updates via WhatsApp and SMS for every dispatch.

What is the TrailHaven 14-day Trail Trial and return policy?

You are welcome to pitch your tent in your living room, pack your rucksack with ballast weight, and test sleeping bag warmth in comfort. If the fit, volume, or dimensions are not right for your torso or trip requirements, return or exchange it within 14 days in clean, unsoiled condition with original tags intact for a 100% refund or instant exchange.

How does the 3-Year TrailHaven Field Warranty work?

Every primary TrailHaven product (tents, backpacks, sleeping bags, and titanium stoves) carries a comprehensive 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects, zipper failures, pole snapping under rated wind conditions, and seam-tape degradation. If accidental rock tearing occurs during an extreme expedition, our Chennai workshop offers subsidized lifetime repairs with original Cordura fabric patches.

Do you offer discounts for educational trekking clubs and NCC cadets?

Yes. We believe youth mountaineering creates environmentally conscious citizens. Accredited university mountaineering clubs, Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) trainees, and certified mountain guides are eligible for a 15% institutional discount across all expedition hardware. Please submit your request via the form above with the subject "Bulk / Expedition Group Order".
