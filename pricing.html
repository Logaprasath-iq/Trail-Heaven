Bundle FAQs & Ordering Guidelines

Are these packages recurring subscription fees?

No! These are one-time physical product bundles. You own all the hardware outright. There are no memberships, software fees, or automatic recurring charges.

Can I swap or customize an item in a package?

Yes. If you wish to swap tent sizes or upgrade your sleeping pad within a package, simply reach out to our Chennai technical advisors via WhatsApp before placing your order for a customized order voucher.

How are package bundles dispatched?

All package components are carefully inspected, pre-assembled, and dispatched together in heavy-duty weatherproof carton crates directly from our Chennai fulfillment hub.

Not Sure Which Bundle Fits Your Expedition?

Message our field advisors with your destination, duration, and elevation, and we'll configure your ideal setup.
