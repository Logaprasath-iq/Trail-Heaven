Behind the Lens: Mountain Photography Ethics

Capturing the raw power of the Himalayas and Western Ghats requires more than great optics. Our field visual teams follow strict non-invasive ethics to protect the environments we document.

Sub-Zero Battery Chemistry

At -20°C, lithium camera batteries lose 60% of their effective voltage within minutes. Our photographers keep spare batteries in inner wool chest pockets and sleep with them inside down bags.

Pro Tip: Bring dual 20,000mAh PD powerbanks

Condensation & Moisture Barriers

Moving directly from sub-zero winds into a heated tent causes moisture to condense inside camera elements. We seal cameras in airtight dry sacks before entering tents to let optics warm gradually.

Pro Tip: 45-minute acclimation buffer

Wildlife Distance & Zero Drones

We never use drones over national parks, alpine passes, or shola sanctuaries where whirring blades harass endangered mountain goats (Nilgiri tahr) or golden eagles. Telephotos only from 100m+.

Strict Leave No Trace adherence
