Customer Inquiries

Product Quality & Shopping FAQs

Clear information about materials, warranties, Pan-India deliveries, and field service.

What is TrailHaven's 3-Year Alpine Guarantee?

Every tent, backpack, sleeping bag, and stove is protected by our comprehensive 3-Year Alpine Guarantee. If any seam, zipper, pole ferrule, or harness hardware fails under normal outdoor use, our Chennai service workshop will repair or replace it at zero charge.

How fast does TrailHaven deliver across India?

We dispatch all orders directly from our Chennai Fulfillment Center. Orders to South Indian metros (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi, Chennai) arrive in 24 to 48 hours. Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, and tier-2 cities take 3 to 4 business days via insured express air courier.

Are spare parts like tent poles, stakes, and buckles available separately?

Yes! We maintain an extensive inventory of individual pole sections, Guy-line sets, titanium stakes, and replacement hipbelt buckles. Contact our customer advisory desk on WhatsApp or email to request specific parts.

Can I test equipment before embarking on an expedition?

We strongly advise test pitching any new shelter and test packing your rucksack at home. If the size or torso fit does not suit your anatomy, you can exchange it within 14 days in unused condition.

Need Custom Gear Guidance for Your Next Trek?

Our Chennai-based expedition veterans help you configure the exact sleep system and tent for your destination elevation.
