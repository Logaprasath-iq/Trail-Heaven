Trail Wisdom

Camping Tips & Wilderness Advice

Monsoon Pack Liners

Always line the interior of your rucksack with a heavy-duty contractor trash bag. Raincovers blow off in strong winds; internal liners keep down sleeping bags 100% dry.

Winter Water Bottle Trick

Pour boiling water into a leakproof Nalgene bottle, wrap it in a wool sock, and tuck it into the footbox of your sleeping bag 15 minutes before bed for lasting warmth.

LNT Campfire Rules

Never cut living alpine tree branches. Use a compact titanium canister stove like the Nomad for cooking, leaving delicate meadows completely pristine for local wildlife.
