The TrailHaven Heritage

Forged in the Wilderness. Dedicated to Greater Tomorrows.

TrailHaven is an authentic outdoor engineering collective founded in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We build expedition-class equipment designed to survive the harshest extremes on Earth while leaving zero ecological footprint.

Our Genesis

From a Frustrating Monsoon Trek to India's Premier Technical Gear Brand

In July 2018, three lifelong friends from Chennai set out on a 10-day monsoon traverse through the rugged forests of the Western Ghats. On day three, during a relentless torrential cloudburst, their expensive imported tents suffered catastrophic seam leaks, and their rucksack frames tore under 18-kilo loads.

Sheltered under emergency tarps, they realized that virtually all outdoor equipment sold in India was either flimsy lifestyle imitation gear or prohibitively expensive Western gear ill-suited to tropical humidity and high-abrasion terrains.

Back in Chennai, they pooled their engineering and textile backgrounds to prototype the first TrailHaven shelter. Eight years later, TrailHaven equips mountaineering expeditions, research biologists, and weekend stargazers across the country.
