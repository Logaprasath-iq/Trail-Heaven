Our Conservation Ethic

Crafted in Chennai. Built to Safeguard Wilderness.

Founded in 2018 in Chennai by avid trekkers who were dismayed by brittle imported gear destined for landfills after two seasons, TrailHaven was born on a simple principle: longevity is the ultimate sustainability.

We pledge 1% of all revenue toward trail clean-up initiatives across the Western Ghats and Himalayan bugyals. We offer free lifetime hardware repairs, ensuring no TrailHaven tent or pack is abandoned prematurely.
